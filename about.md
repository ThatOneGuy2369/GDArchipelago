# Geometry Dash Archipelago

Play Geometry Dash as part of an [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) multiworld randomizer.

- **Checks:** beating a level, and the secret coins you grab on that run, are sent automatically.
- **Locks:** levels you haven't unlocked won't start; portals for gamemodes you don't have kill you.
- **Items:** what other players send you pops up in game. Traps apply to your next level.
- **Gauntlets:** report them from the **AP** button on the main menu once you've beaten every level in one.

Set your **Server** and **Slot name** in this mod's settings, then press **AP > Connect**.
Only levels that are part of your seed are affected; everything else plays normally.
